## How payment works {#payment}

Payments are processed by our authorised reseller (Merchant of Record); its name is shown on the checkout page and on your receipt. It acts as the seller: you pay on its secure checkout page, and it issues your receipt and handles taxes, refunds and chargebacks. We never see your card details. The Pass becomes active within seconds after the payment is confirmed.

## When you get a refund {#refund}

Refund requests must be made within **30 days** of the purchase.

**Pass not delivered: full refund.** For example:

- you were charged, but the Pass did not become active or did not work because of a failure on our side;
- you were charged twice, or for something you did not choose.

**Pass you have not used: full refund, no reason needed.**

Some cases are refunded automatically, without a request, and you receive an email: a second Pass for the same email or account, a payment that does not match the order, and a payment confirmed after the account was deleted. Our authorised reseller may also issue refunds under its own policy.

## When there is no refund {#no-refund}

- A Pass that has been used, unless it was not delivered or did not work as described.
- Requests made more than 30 days after the purchase.

Nothing in this policy limits the rights you have under the mandatory consumer law of your country. If you are a consumer in the EU/EEA or the UK, you have a 14-day right of withdrawal unless, before paying, you expressly agreed that delivery starts immediately and acknowledged that you lose this right once it starts (see the [Terms of Use](/terms/)).

## After a refund {#after}

A refund or chargeback withdraws the product: the Pass is turned off.

## How to ask for a refund {#how}

Write to support@time2fest.com from the email used for payment, with the date of purchase or the order number from your receipt. We answer within 3 business days. The refund goes back to your original payment method; how soon it appears depends on your bank.

Please contact us before opening a dispute with your bank: it is usually faster.
