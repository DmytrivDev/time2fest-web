# Cookies

- **id:** `cookies` · **path:** `/cookies/`
- **tab:** Cookies · **about:** what is stored in your browser
- **updated:** 26 November 2026

**In short.** Today the site sets no cookies at all: the only thing stored in your browser is your language choice, so no consent banner is needed. When sign-in, chat and live streams open in December 2026, the site will use only necessary cookies and local storage, plus one optional item: video from Facebook, loaded only with your consent. We use no analytics or marketing cookies.

## What is stored today {#today}

| Name | Type | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| t2f:lang | local storage | so the site opens in the language you chose and doesn’t suggest it again | until you clear the site data |

This entry is needed for the site to work, contains no personal data, is not sent to us and is not used for tracking. There are no cookies on the site today.

## Statistics without cookies {#analytics}

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) for details.

## When sign-in, chat and streams open {#later}

These are necessary for the site to work and are always on:

| Name | Type | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| __Secure-t2f.session_token | cookie | keeps you signed in | 60 days, renewed while you use the site |
| __Host-t2f_device | cookie | a random device identifier: keeps your guest data, a Pass bought on this device and device bans | 400 days |
| sign-in cookie | cookie | used only during “Sign in with Google”, to protect against forged requests | short-lived |
| t2f_map_hint | cookie | remembers that you have seen the map hint | 1 year |
| t2f: keys | local and session storage | your settings, your cookie choice, chat preferences, notifications and similar site data; session storage is cleared when you close the tab | until you clear the site data |

Local storage stays on your device unless a feature sends it to our server, for example your schedule when you are signed in.

## Optional: video from Facebook {#optional}

Facebook video is used only as a backup source when a celebration is not available on YouTube. It is loaded only if you agree in the cookie banner or in “Cookie settings”, and you can withdraw your consent there at any time. Without your consent the Facebook player is not loaded, and Facebook sets no cookies through our site.

The YouTube player uses the privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com): YouTube does not set cookies until you play a video; after that, YouTube’s own policy applies. We use no analytics or marketing cookies, so there are no such categories to choose.

## How to delete what is stored {#change}

Clear the site data for time2fest.com in your browser settings. Today the site will then suggest a language based on your browser settings again; once sign-in is open, clearing the data also signs you out on that device.
