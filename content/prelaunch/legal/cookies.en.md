# Cookies

- **id:** `cookies` · **path:** `/cookies/`
- **tab:** Cookies · **about:** what is stored in your browser
- **updated:** December 1, 2026

**In short.** We don’t set cookies for statistics or advertising, which is why there is no consent banner. The only thing stored in your browser is your language choice.

## What is stored in your browser {#list}

| What | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| [[entry name]] — language choice | so the site opens in the language you chose and doesn’t ask again | [[period, or “until you clear the site data”]] |
| [[entry name — if the site remembers your country in “When will the New Year reach you?”; otherwise remove this row]] | so you don’t have to pick your country again | [[period]] |
| [[`__cf_bm` — only if this cookie is actually set on the deployed site; otherwise remove this row]] | a technical cookie from our hosting provider, Cloudflare, used for bot protection | up to 30 minutes |

These entries are needed for the site to work, contain no personal data and are not used for tracking.

## Statistics without cookies {#analytics}

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) for details.

## Video {#video}

The video about the project is hosted on YouTube. Until you press play, YouTube stores nothing in your browser. Once the video starts, YouTube (Google) may set its own cookies.

## How to delete what is stored {#change}

Clear the site data for time2fest.com in your browser settings. The site will then suggest a language based on your browser settings again.

## What will change later {#later}

When sign-in, payments and live streams arrive, new cookies will come with them. We will update this document then and, where required, ask for your consent.
