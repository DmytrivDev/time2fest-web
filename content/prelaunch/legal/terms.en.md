The New Year Pass is a one-time purchase of **$7** (taxes included) that adds extra features for one celebration: New Year 2026/27. It is not a subscription and does not renew.

The Pass is valid from purchase until **1 January 2027, 14:00 UTC**, two hours after the last midnight on Earth. It does not cover other holidays, and it is not sold after the celebration ends.

The Pass includes, as shown on the Pass page at the moment of purchase:

- a Pass badge next to your nickname in the chat and your visible “path”: the countries where you met the New Year;

- quiet chat mode;

If you buy as a guest, the Pass works on that device and is attached once to the first account you sign in to on it, or to the account with the email used for payment, whichever comes first.
