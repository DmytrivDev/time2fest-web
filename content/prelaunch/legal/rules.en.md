# Chat & Community Rules

- **id:** `rules` · **path:** `/rules/`
- **tab:** Chat Rules · **about:** how to behave in the chat and how we moderate it
- **updated:** 26 November 2026

**In short.** The chat is a New Year party with people from all over the world. Greet, celebrate, share how midnight looks where you are. Be kind, and keep links, ads and personal details out. The chat opens for New Year 2026/27 (December 2026). These rules apply to chat messages, nicknames and flags and are part of the Terms of Use.

## Welcome {#welcome}

- Greetings in any language, impressions of the shows, New Year traditions of your country, friendly conversation.
- Reactions and replies.

## Not allowed {#not-allowed}

1. **Hate and harassment:** insults, threats, bullying, attacks on people because of their nationality, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or other characteristics.
2. **Violence:** threats, calls to violence, glorification of violence, terrorism or war crimes.
3. **Sexual content** of any kind, and any content that sexualises minors.
4. **Illegal content** and calls to break the law.
5. **Links, contact details and personal data:** links of any kind, phone numbers, emails, messenger or social media handles; personal data of other people.
6. **Advertising and promotion** of products, services, channels, accounts or people.
7. **Spam and flooding:** repeated messages, mass identical messages, scripts and bots.
8. **Impersonation:** pretending to be another person, Time2Fest or its team.
9. **Seeking dates or private meetings.** Time2Fest is not a dating service.
10. **Fraud and scams**, including requests for money.

Nicknames follow the same rules.

## How we moderate {#moderation}

- Every message and nickname is checked automatically by rule-based filters and by an AI model (Anthropic), in all languages. A flagged message is hidden for everyone.
- The first messages of a new participant, and messages that look suspicious, may be held until checked: you see your own message marked “Under review”, others do not see it yet.
- When the chat is busy, slow mode limits how often you can write. At night, when the site owner is not watching, the rules are applied more strictly.
- When the AI is unavailable, a stricter built-in filter is used.
- For Pass holders, messages are translated automatically; translations are checked too.
- Automatic moderation makes mistakes in both directions. If something was hidden by mistake, tell us.

## Reporting {#reporting}

Use “Report” in a message’s menu to report a message or its author. Reports go to a moderation queue reviewed by the site owner; many reports on one message may hide it automatically. You can also write to support@time2fest.com.

**Illegal content.** Anyone, even without an account, can report content they believe is illegal by email to support@time2fest.com. Please include where it is (room, approximate time, nickname), why you think it is illegal, your name and email (not needed for reports of child sexual abuse material), and a statement that the report is made in good faith. We confirm receipt, decide promptly and tell you the outcome. Content involving a threat to someone’s life or safety may be reported to the authorities.

## Consequences {#consequences}

Depending on how serious the violation is:

- the message is hidden;
- your messages are held for review;
- a ban from writing in the chat, for an account, a guest or a device. Bans are indefinite unless we lift them; a device ban stays after the account is deleted; for up to 24 hours, new guests from the same network may also be blocked.

A ban does not stop you from watching the streams.

## Appeals {#appeals}

If you think a decision was wrong, write to support@time2fest.com with your nickname and the approximate time. A person will review it, free of charge. When we hide your content or ban you, we tell you the main reason, unless that would help someone get around the rules. You may also have the right to go to an out-of-court dispute settlement body or a court in your country.
