# Privacy Policy

- **id:** `privacy` · **path:** `/privacy/`
- **tab:** Privacy Policy · **about:** what data we collect and why
- **updated:** 26 November 2026

**In short.** We collect as little as we can. Today the site has no sign-in, payments or chat, so we know almost nothing about you: we count visits without cookies and see your email only if you write to us. When sign-in, chat, live streams and Pass sales open in December 2026, you can still watch without an account; to chat you choose a nickname and a flag, and an account needs only an email. We do not store your raw IP address, we do not sell data and we show no ads. Chat moderation and translation use one AI provider, Anthropic, which receives only the text.

## Who we are {#who}

time2fest.com is run by Time2Fest, based in Ukraine (“we”, “us”). We are responsible (the controller) for the data described below. Contact for any privacy question or request: support@time2fest.com.

## On this site today {#today}

Today time2fest.com is a preview: a countdown, the celebration schedule, country pages, and information about the New Year Pass and its price. There is no sign-in, payment, chat or live streaming yet. Today we receive only the following:

- **Technical visit data.** When you open a page, your browser sends our hosting provider, Cloudflare, your IP address, browser and device type, the page address and the time of the request. A page can’t be delivered without this; the same data helps protect the site from attacks.
- **Visit statistics.** We use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and doesn’t follow you across other sites. We only see overall numbers: page views, countries, referring sites, device and browser types.
- **Emails.** If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. Messages to our addresses are received by Cloudflare Email Routing and forwarded to our mailbox.
- **Settings on your device.** Your language choice is stored only in your browser and is not sent to us. See [Cookies](/cookies/).

Midnight “in your time” is calculated in your browser; your time zone is not sent to us. Calendar files are also created on your device. The legal bases are our legitimate interest in running a secure site and understanding which pages are read (in aggregate only), and replying to you when you ask us something.

The rest of this policy describes how the site works when sign-in, chat, live streams and Pass sales are open.

We do not collect your date of birth, phone number, postal address or payment card details, and we do not need any special categories of data. If you choose a flag or nickname that reveals something about you, it is public by your own choice.

**IP address.** We use your IP address only to determine your country (to suggest a flag and language and to count viewers by country in aggregate) and as the daily network fingerprint described above. We do not store the raw IP, and neither the IP nor the fingerprint is linked to your messages, profile or orders.

**Where the data comes from.** Most data comes from you. Google gives us your account email, name and picture link if you sign in with Google; the payment provider tells us whether your payment was completed or refunded (not your card details); Cloudflare tells our server the country of your IP address.

**Do you have to give us data?** No. You can watch without giving us anything. A nickname and flag are needed only to chat, an email only to create an account or to pay.

We have weighed them against your interests: we use as little data as possible, keep it briefly and never use it for advertising. You can object to this processing (see “Your rights”).

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you solely by automated means. Automatic moderation can hide or hold a message; you can ask a person to review it.

## Cookies and local storage {#cookies}

When sign-in, chat and streams open, the site uses only necessary cookies and local storage (to keep you signed in, to recognise your device and to remember your settings) and one optional item: video from Facebook, loaded only with your consent. We use no analytics or marketing cookies. The full list is in [Cookies](/cookies/).

## Moderation and AI {#ai}

To keep the chat safe, every message and nickname is checked by automatic rules and by an AI model (Anthropic Claude). The AI receives only the text and its language, never your email, account ID or IP. Translation, a Pass feature, also sends only the message text to Anthropic. If the AI is unavailable, a stricter built-in filter on our server is used and translation is temporarily unavailable. You can ask for a human review of a moderation decision at support@time2fest.com.

## Your rights {#rights}

You have the right to access your data and get a copy, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time; withdrawing consent does not affect processing done before.

**Right to object.** You can object at any time, on grounds relating to your situation, to processing based on our legitimate interests. We will stop unless we have compelling legitimate grounds, for example a ban needed to keep the chat safe.

To get a copy of your data or to delete it, write to support@time2fest.com. When you delete your account, your account data is erased or anonymised; your chat messages stay visible with the author shown as “deleted” until their normal deletion date; order records are kept without your identity for 3 years; a device ban stays in place.

We answer within one month (in complex cases we may extend this by up to two more months and will tell you why). We may ask you to confirm the request from the email linked to your account. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in the EU, the authority of your country; in Ukraine, the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights.

## Children {#children}

The chat and accounts are not intended for children under 13 (under 16 in the EU/EEA). Purchases are for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children below these ages; if you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

## Security {#security}

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS); our server is reachable only through Cloudflare; session cookies are secure and not readable by scripts; personal data is never put in page addresses; logs contain no email, codes, tokens or IP; admin access is protected with two-factor authentication.

## Changes and contact {#changes}

We will update this policy when our processing changes. The date at the top shows the latest version; important changes will be announced on the site and by email to account holders. Questions and requests: support@time2fest.com.
